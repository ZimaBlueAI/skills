# Editing recipes

## Interview to a vertical short

Log each source and transcript with timecodes. Select one complete thought and establish the claim's context; create an audio-first cut with natural pauses. Choose the opening from actual content, then add reaction shots or relevant cleared B-roll where it helps understanding. Reframe to keep the speaker and necessary context visible. Check each subtitle against the words actually spoken and view on a small display. Deliver the cut and source/timecode notes.

## Music video from supplied footage

Lock the supplied master track and its start position. Mark phrase changes, emphasis and narrative beats; vary visual pacing rather than cutting mechanically on every beat. Keep song timing and lyric meaning intact. Verify the first and last frames, transitions and audio sync on the final rendered file. Note whether lip sync was actually inspected; do not imply the source performance was generated by the supplied master track.

## Documentary or explanatory recut

Map each factual claim to a source; preserve original quotation context and distinguish archival material, reenactment and generated footage. Seek the needed evidence before adding illustrative B-roll. Label unresolved factual or rights gaps. Check whether juxtaposition implies a false chronology or causation. Use the research reference for current claims and rights.

## Feedback or missing tools

Translate each requested revision into a timecoded change, preserve previous versions, and compare the affected boundaries. If the Bot lacks a usable renderer, deliver an explicitly unrendered EDL plus the missing dependency and next action; do not label a plan or screenshot as a finished video.
