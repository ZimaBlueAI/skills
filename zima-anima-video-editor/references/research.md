# Search and evidence patterns

Use a small search matrix adapted to the task:

- Product operation: `official product feature documentation`, then inspect version, release date and exact limitations.
- Editing technique: `ffmpeg official documentation <filter>`, then verify on a short real sample.
- Footage: `<subject> original footage license attribution`; open the actual rights page and the individual asset page, not just a platform's generic marketing page.
- News clip: `<claim> original statement date`, then compare two independent sources and label uncertainty.
- Music: `<track> creator license commercial use sync rights`; streaming access does not establish sync or distribution rights.
- Social trend: search names, date window and original posts; separate direct evidence from commentary and verify context.

Evidence note: claim or intended use; source title and direct URL; publisher/creator; publication and access dates; exact finding; relevant license and constraints; confidence or unresolved issue. For a disputed factual claim, retain the counterevidence. Prefer short summaries to extensive quotation. Don't claim an external fact is current if you couldn't check it.
